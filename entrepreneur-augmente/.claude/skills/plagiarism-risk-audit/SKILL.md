# plagiarism-risk-audit

1. Compare intra-manuscript and available corpora.
2. Record exact/near-verbatim/paraphrase/structural matches.
3. Attach provenance and attribution status.
4. Report false-positive risk.
5. Escalate suspected plagiarism to human review.
